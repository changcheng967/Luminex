# Luminex NNUE v2 — program status

Status: **in progress**. v2 = the shipped v1 (see V1-GEN768.md) plus the
levers below, decided by measurement (G0 battery results and the six-arm
comparison), not by preference. The external design documents that fed this
(round 1 and round 2 proposals) live outside the repo; the decisions are
recorded here.

## Settled (all function-preserving warm starts, tested)

- int16 accumulator made exact by training-time range constraints, retiring
  the saturating fallback and its silent-corruption mode.
- Eight material-phase output buckets over the whole tail, zero per-node
  cost; bucket tails seed as copies of the plain tail (exact warm start).
- PSQT-style linear path as a rider (the G0 linear share is a minority:
  R2_lin 0.196 vs R2_full 0.515).
- Rounded requantization ((v + 32) >> 6), removing the half-LSB bias.

## Gated on the arm comparison (baseline / buckets / J32 / pairwise /
pairwise+J32 / over-capacity probe)

- Tail width J 16 -> 32.
- Pairwise activation (FT halves multiplied; duplicated halves reproduce
  SCReLU exactly, antisymmetric noise 0.0005 to break symmetry).
- P/N/K king-independent threat features (O(1) flips per move).

## Evidence anchors

- Interactions dominate: ridge linear head over the frozen accumulator
  reaches only 0.38 of the full net's R2.
- Affine shrinkage a = 1.18 with a heavy tail: worst 10% of positions carry
  78% of squared error; a monotone rescale does not change minimax move
  choice, so this is a loss-side matter, not a post-hoc knob.
- The overfit catastrophe (105 epochs over 50M positions: train MAE 91.1,
  0-150-150 at fixed nodes) set the epoch and validation discipline.

## Open

- Alpha (eval share of node time) via evalbench; converts eval-cost ratios
  into Elo.
- Round-2 proposals under evaluation: second feature transformer feeding L3
  directly (two instructions per lane of rank versus 96 per rank through the
  dense tail), quiet-only or quiet-weighted loss, structural predicate
  features (passed pawns, shelter).
