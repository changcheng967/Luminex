## Known Issues
1. **Board state desynchronization**: Luminex's internal board differs from GUI's board after move application
   - Moves like a5b5 (horizontal rook) and c7c6 (backward pawn) are generated by Luminex but rejected by GUI
   - Root cause: GUI and Luminex have different board states after applying the same move list
2. **king_square corruption**: Fixed by initializing to SQUARE_NONE in set()
3. **State stack overflow**: Added bounds check to prevent buffer overflow
4. **Remaining illegal moves**: 3-7 per 20 games (down from 100% originally)
